Automatic renewal: subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period (monthly or annual) and your payment method will be charged at the then-current rate, until you cancel. An account administrator can cancel renewal at any time through the ScopeSnap website billing page and the secure Stripe portal, or email support@scopesnap.com.au for help. New accounts start with a 14-day Starter trial with one user and 5 jobs they create across the trial, plus a signup sample job that does not count against the job allowance. Photos never consume the other AI allowance. The Starter per-job limits apply: 20 saved photos and up to 25 photo analyses on one job, within the 30-analysis total for the whole trial. These trial allowances do not reset monthly. A voice workflow can use multiple requests; successful retries count; failed provider requests are refunded, with a separate retry cap. No credit card is required. Choosing a paid subscription starts paid billing at checkout, even if time remains on your free trial.

Except where required by applicable law, including non-excludable Australian and New Zealand rights, unused parts of billing periods are not refunded. We may change our prices by giving active subscribers at least 30 days’ notice by email. A price change takes effect from your next billing period after the notice period ends, and you can cancel before then if you don’t want to continue at the new price.

Photo, voice and AI allowances

Starter / Professional / Business allow 20 / 40 / 60 saved photos, 25 / 50 / 75 photo analyses and 10 / 15 / 20 voice recordings per job. The photo-analysis allowance includes 5 / 10 / 15 extra analyses for retakes. Deleting a photo frees a saved-photo slot but does not refund completed AI analyses. Uploaded plans and documents count toward storage but do not consume photo slots. The app limits each recording to five minutes; the server also caps audio upload size and processing requests. Voice processing allows up to two passes per included recording. Each job includes three generations of each report (the first version and two updates), three briefings, three Scope Checks, three scope-item breakdowns and one Plan Scan. Company-wide monthly AI allowances equal the per-job allowance multiplied by included jobs: 200 / 750 / 2,250 photo analyses for Starter / Professional / Business. Each Business 10-job pack adds 750 photo analyses per month, with the same 75-analysis limit per job. This includes 8 / 15 / 30 Plan Scans per month for Starter / Professional / Business, with one Plan Scan per job. Work on existing jobs also uses these monthly allowances. Per-job AI limits do not reset monthly. Failed provider requests are released from the displayed allowance, but repeated failed attempts are capped. Saved reports can be reopened or downloaded without using AI.

Business customers can add packs of 10 jobs for AUD $59/month plus applicable tax per pack, billed separately monthly even when the main plan is annual. Each pack adds Business AI capacity for 10 jobs and 10 Plan Scans monthly; existing per-job limits still apply. Capacity is granted only after confirmed payment. Allowances reset on the first day of the UTC calendar month while paid access is active, and unused allowance does not roll over. Add-ons renew until cancelled; cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid add-on period. Increases are charged for the remaining billing period. To reduce packs, cancel renewal and choose fewer after expiry. No automatic overage charges apply. Extra job packs require a Business subscription and do not increase seats or storage.
