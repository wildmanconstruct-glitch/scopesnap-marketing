How it works

Walk the site.
Leave with the scope.

Whether it's a new build, extension, renovation or repair, start a job and capture the work on site. Keep photos, voice notes and measurements together by room or area. No plans needed.

New builds · Extensions · Renovations · Repairs & maintenance

Start free trial → See the steps ↓
01 / Create the job

Start with the rooms.
Not the paperwork.

Choose the job type, then add the rooms or work areas you need — whether you're documenting a new build, extension, renovation or maintenance job.

No drawings required. Add more rooms as you go.

02 / Photos and AI

Keep every site photo
with the right room.

Take photos of the work as you go, room by room or area by area. Each photo stays with the right place and can appear in the finished report.

Open the AI brief to see observations worth checking, then review the notes yourself.

03 / AI Voice Capture

Talk through the work.
Let the notes take shape.

Talk naturally about new work, alterations, repairs, materials and anything that needs confirmation.

ScopeSnap helps turn the recording into an editable written scope — you approve the wording.

04 / Tape Measure

Measurements captured.
While you're standing there.

Use the camera-assisted Tape Measure with a known reference to estimate distances, then save them against the room. Enter exact dimensions manually whenever needed.

Camera readings are approximate. Critical dimensions and set-out still need checking on site.

05 / Review your capture

From site visit
to a scope you can check.

Review the room details, photos and measurements. ScopeSnap brings your capture into the client and internal report process.

You review the scope before sharing it.

06 / Professional reports

Two detailed PDFs.
Ready for the right people.

Share a photo-rich client report, or generate a detailed internal version with estimating quantities, room measurements and site checks.

View the complete client report ↗ or internal report ↗.

OPTIONAL / PLAN SCAN

Have a floor plan?
Put it to use.

If you already have a plan, Plan Scan can suggest rooms and notes for you to review before adding them. It’s a useful shortcut when drawings exist, but never a requirement to start the job.
