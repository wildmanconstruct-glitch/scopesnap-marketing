How it works

Walk the site.
Leave with the scope.

ScopeSnap keeps your plans, photos, measurements and notes together by room. Here’s what the walkthrough looks like in the app.

Start free trial → See the steps ↓
01 / PlanScan

Start with the plans.
Not a blank page.

Upload a plan and review the rooms ScopeSnap identifies. Keep, edit or skip rooms before they are added to the job.

Prefer to start on site? Add rooms yourself in a few taps.

02 / Photos and AI

Keep every site photo
with the right room.

Take photos as you go, room by room. Each photo is connected to its location and can appear in the finished report.

Open the AI brief to see observations worth checking, then review the notes yourself.

03 / AI Voice Capture

Talk through the work.
Let the notes take shape.

Speak naturally while you inspect. Capture strip-out, layout changes, materials and the things that need confirmation.

ScopeSnap helps turn the recording into an editable written scope — you approve the wording.

04 / Tape Measure

Measurements captured.
While you're standing there.

Use the camera-assisted Tape Measure with a known reference to estimate distances, then save them against the room. Enter exact dimensions manually whenever needed.

Camera readings are approximate. Critical dimensions and set-out still need checking on site.

05 / Job overview

One place to review.
Nothing lost along the way.

Review rooms, notes, measurements and photography from one connected job record.

Refine the draft and check everything before preparing the scope.

06 / Professional reports

Two detailed PDFs.
Ready for the right people.

Share a photo-rich client report, or generate a detailed internal version with estimating quantities, room measurements and site checks.

View the complete client report ↗ or internal report ↗.
