How it works

One site visit.
Five steps.

Whether it's a new build, extension, renovation or repair, start a job and capture the work on site. Keep photos, voice notes and measurements together by room or area. No plans needed.

New builds · Extensions · Granny flats · Renovations

01 — WORKFLOW

FROM FIRST VISIT
TO FINISHED REPORT.

One job, start to finish. Start your way, capture on site, answer what matters, review, then send the reports.

  1. STEP 1 — START

    START
    YOUR WAY.

    Quick Create when you're already on site: address and client, then straight into capture. Or Full Setup with the appointment, project details and office notes before you go. Got plans? Scan them to add rooms. No plans? No problem.

    • Quick Create
    • Full Setup
    • Plans optional
  2. STEP 2 — CAPTURE

    CAPTURE
    ON SITE.

    Walk it room by room. Photos with ✦ AI notes, voice notes and measurements, all saved to the right room.

    • Photos
    • Voice
    • Measurements
  3. STEP 3 — QUESTIONS

    NOTHING
    MISSED.

    Guided questions for every room and the whole house: layout, finishes, services, what's by owner and what still needs confirming. Talk it through and ScopeSnap fills in the answers for you to check.

    • Room questions
    • Whole house
    • By owner
  4. STEP 4 — REVIEW

    REVIEW
    YOUR SCOPE.

    Everything you captured is drafted into clear scope wording, room by room. Edit anything, then run Scope Check to catch gaps before you send.

    • ✦ AI draft
    • Edit
    • Scope Check
  5. STEP 5 — REPORTS

    SEND THE
    REPORTS.

    Create a branded Client Report and a detailed Internal Report from the same site visit, ready to send before you're back at the office.
