How it works

Walk the site.
Leave with the scope.

ScopeSnap keeps your plans, photos, measurements and notes together by room. Here’s what the walkthrough looks like in the app.

Start free trial → See the steps ↓
01 / Set up the job

Start with the plans.
Or add rooms yourself.

Add the client and address, then choose the rooms you’ll inspect. If you have a floor plan, Plan Scan can pick out rooms and notes for you to review before adding them.

You stay in control of what goes into the job.

02 / Capture on site

Keep the photos
with the room.

Work through each room with your phone. Take photos, add measurements, confirm selections and record a voice note as you describe the work. Everything stays attached to the right room.

AI can turn the recording into a draft scope for you to check and edit.

03 / Review the scope

Make the draft
your own.

Read the proposed work room by room. Edit the wording, add what’s missing and check any details that still need a decision before you share it.

Your company scoping rules can remind the AI about the things your team always checks.

04 / Share the report

Put your name
on a clear PDF.

Generate a report with your business branding, the room details, measurements, proposed work and reference photos. Use the client or internal version for the people who need it.
