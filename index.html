Photo AI includes 5 / 10 / 15 extra analyses per job on Solo / Professional / Business for retakes. Deleting a photo frees a saved-photo slot but does not refund completed AI analyses. Uploaded plans and documents use storage, not photo slots.

Each job includes a client report and an internal report, plus two updates to each. Voice recordings can be up to five minutes long, with up to two processing passes per included recording.

Monthly allowances reset on the 1st of each month, including annual plans. In Australia, the reset happens in the morning. Per-job AI allowances last for the life of the job. Deleting a job does not restore its job allowance. Monthly AI limits also apply to work on older jobs.

Reopening or downloading saved work uses no AI. Failed provider requests are refunded; successful retries count and separate retry caps apply. AI requires internet and your permission. Declining AI permission does not change the subscription price.

Paid photo uploads are limited to 5 MB after compression; other files to 25 MB. Your company storage allowance also applies.
