The problem

The problem starts after the site walk.

You leave with hundreds of photos, scattered notes and half the job still in your head. By the time the quote gets written, details get missed.

Without ScopeSnap
  • Photos buried in your camera roll
  • Notes spread across messages and voice memos
  • Scope written from memory
  • Missed items found mid-build
  • Messy handover to clients or trades
With ScopeSnap
  • Photos, notes and voice captured in one place
  • Everything grouped by room
  • AI cleans up your notes
  • Clear scope record before you leave
  • Easier quoting and cleaner handover
Features

Built for site walks, not office admin.

Organised by room

Every photo, note and voice recording stays attached to the room or area it belongs to. No sorting later.

Built for fast site walks

Capture what you see while you walk instead of typing everything up later back at the office.

Talk it through. ScopeSnap writes it up.

Speak naturally as you walk the job. ScopeSnap strips the filler and turns it into clean, usable scope notes.

Clear reports

How it works

Four steps. Scope done.

1

Open the job

Choose the room or area you're standing in. Add rooms as you go.

2

Capture as you walk

Take photos, add notes or talk through what you see. Everything tags to the current room.

3

ScopeSnap organises it

Everything is grouped by room and cleaned into usable scope notes automatically.

4

Export a clear scope

Create a report you can quote from, send to the client or hand to your team. Before you leave site.
