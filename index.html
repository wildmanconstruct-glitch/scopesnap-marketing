Features

Everything you need on site, nothing you don't.

Designed around how builders actually work — fast, messy, on a phone, in the middle of a job.

Room-by-room photo capture

Take photos room by room and they stay organised — no more scrolling through 300 shots trying to remember which bathroom that was.

Voice notes, cleaned up by AI

Talk through what you see on site. ScopeSnap transcribes it, removes the filler words, and turns it into clean professional observations.

AI scope report generation

When you're done walking the site, generate a professional scope report. Client version, trade breakdown, or internal notes — all from the same capture.

Guided inspection workflows

Select the job type first — renovation, extension, defect, maintenance, new build — and ScopeSnap shapes the questions and flow around that project.

Team access & job sharing

Your whole team sees the same jobs. Sales reps, site supervisors, estimators — everyone works from the same captured information.

PDF scope reports

Export clean PDF reports for clients, trades, or your own records. Professional enough to hand to a homeowner, detailed enough for your estimator.

Use cases

Works for every type of inspection.

Whether you're scoping a full renovation or logging a defect, ScopeSnap adapts to the job type.

Renovations

Full home renovation scopes — room by room, trade by trade. Capture every detail from structural to finishes.

"3BR renovation — kitchen, 2 bathrooms, laundry, repaint throughout, new flooring"

Extensions & additions

Scope new additions, second storeys, granny flats and outbuildings. Document existing conditions and new works clearly.

"Rear extension — new slab, framing, roof, windows, tie-in to existing structure"

Defect inspections

Log defects with photos, descriptions and priority levels. Generate clear defect schedules for builders and clients.

"Settlement cracking to render, garage door alignment, missing roof flashing at valley"

Maintenance & repairs

Capture maintenance issues across a property. Create itemised schedules for approval and trade allocation.

"Annual maintenance — gutters, silicone, touch-up paint, gate latch, tap washer replacement"

Real estate & property management

Condition reports, ingoing/outgoing inspections, and maintenance schedules for property managers and buyers agents.

"Pre-purchase inspection — roof, subfloor, electrical, plumbing, structural overview"

New builds

Practical completion inspections, stage inspections and handover walkthroughs for new construction projects.

"Practical completion — defect list, missing items, final fixtures, landscaping sign-off"

Why we built this

Built by a builder, for builders.

ScopeSnap started from a real frustration — leaving a site with 200 unorganised photos, scattered voice memos and notes that meant nothing three days later. By the time the scope was written, things were missing. Variations followed. Clients asked questions that should have been answered at the walkthrough.

So we built the tool we wished existed. Room-by-room. Photo-first. Voice notes that actually turn into something useful. AI that generates the scope so you can focus on the job, not the paperwork.

ScopeSnap is being built alongside active renovation and inspection projects — not in a boardroom. Every feature exists because a real site visit made it obvious it was needed.
