How it works

One walkthrough.
Clear scope before you leave.

Four steps. No office. No chasing your own notes.

1 — Set up the job

Create a new job and add rooms as you walk the site. Everything stays organised from the start.

2 — Capture as you walk

Take photos and talk through what you see. Each photo and voice note is attached to the room automatically.

3 — AI writes the scope

Your voice notes become clean written scope, room by room. No transcription, no editing — just review and adjust.

4 — Share the report

Export a clear scope report. Send it to the client, attach it to a quote, or hand it to your trades.

Every missed item is a variation you won’t recover.
